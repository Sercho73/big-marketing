# Atlas Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 September 2026_

Atlas is a personal, non-commercial financial analysis application intended for use by its owner only.

## Data accessed

Atlas may access read-only banking information through regulated Open Banking providers such as Enable Banking. This may include account details, balances, and transaction history.

Atlas does not initiate payments, transfers, card operations, or any other banking transaction.

## Purpose

Banking data is used only to provide personal financial analysis, including expense and income tracking, categorisation, historical analysis, anomaly detection, alerts, and related personal finance features.

## Data sharing

Atlas does not sell personal or financial data. Data may be processed by infrastructure or service providers only when necessary to operate the application, subject to appropriate security and data-minimisation practices.

## Security and data minimisation

Atlas is designed to minimise the amount of financial information shared with external services. Banking credentials are not stored by Atlas. Access to bank data is provided through the user's explicit Open Banking consent and can be revoked through the relevant bank or Open Banking provider.

## Retention

Financial data may be retained for the purpose of maintaining the user's personal historical records, backups, and analysis. The user remains in control of the application and its data.

## Contact

For privacy matters, use the contact email registered with the Atlas application or its Open Banking provider.
