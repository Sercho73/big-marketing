# Atlas Terms of Use

_Last updated: 11 September 2026_

Atlas is a personal, non-commercial financial analysis application intended for use by its owner only.

## Scope

Atlas provides read-only access to financial information supplied by the user's bank or authorised Open Banking providers. It is intended to help the user review transactions, analyse spending and income, identify patterns, and receive informational alerts.

## No banking operations

Atlas does not initiate payments, transfers, card operations, or any other banking transaction. Any financial action must be completed directly through the user's bank or another authorised financial service.

## Informational purpose

Any categorisation, anomaly detection, forecasts, alerts, or AI-generated explanations provided by Atlas are informational only. The user remains responsible for reviewing financial information and making any financial or banking decisions.

## Availability and accuracy

Atlas is designed to process data supplied by banks and third-party Open Banking providers, but uninterrupted availability or absolute accuracy cannot be guaranteed. Source data may be delayed, incomplete, or changed by the bank or provider.

## Personal use

Atlas is not offered as a public financial service and is not intended for use by third parties unless its terms, privacy controls, and regulatory requirements are reviewed and updated accordingly.

## Changes

These terms may be updated as Atlas evolves. The version applicable at any time is the version published on this page.
